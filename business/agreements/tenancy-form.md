## The tenancy in plain words

In this form "we" and "Mwavuli" mean Mwavuli Holdings Ltd, acting for the landlord, and "you" means the tenant.

### 1. Who we are

This tenancy is between the landlord and you. We act for the landlord: we collect the rent and look after the
building.

### 2. Paying your rent

Pay your rent to us only, by mobile money or bank, never cash to our staff. Your rent is due on the day written
above, each month, unless we move your due date (clause 12). Your month runs from one due date to the next. Pay to
the mobile money number or the bank account below.

- **Mobile money number:** [mobile money number]
- **Bank:** [bank]
- **Account name:** [account name]
- **Account number:** [account number]

### 3. Your deposit

If you are a new tenant, your deposit is paid to us and kept in a separate account. We use it only for what this
tenancy allows (unpaid rent, damage, legal costs), and the rest goes back to you.

If you were already living in the building, the landlord keeps the deposit already held (the amount is written
above) and gives it back to you.

### 4. Rent paid ahead

If you pay rent ahead, we hold it in a separate account, like deposits, and pay it to the landlord month by
month on the 1st. What we have not yet paid to the landlord stays there. When your tenancy ends, what is still held
for the months that start after it goes back to you, with no fee, within 14 days of our visit to the unit (clause
10). What you still owe is taken from your deposit first, if we hold it, and then from this rent. A month that has
started is paid in full.

### 5. If you do not pay

If you miss your due date, we remind you, call you and visit you, then send a written notice. Should the matter
need to go to court, the case is brought in the landlord's name at their approval. Any late fee goes to us.

- **Late fee:** [amount]
- **When it applies:** [when it applies; 2 lines]

### 6. Repairs

Tell us by phone or WhatsApp if something is broken or unsafe, and we arrange a trusted contractor.

- **Phone or WhatsApp:** [phone or WhatsApp number]

You pay in full for damage you cause, as soon as the repairs are done. If you do not, it is taken from your
deposit (clause 3).

### 7. Electricity, water and garbage

Your share is charged at cost, with no markup. We use the meter reading. If your unit has no meter, we agree a
fair share or a fixed amount with you.

- **Meter number:** [meter number]
- **Or share or amount agreed:** [share or amount agreed]

### 8. Privacy

Landlord and tenant details are used only to manage the building and are not sold or shared otherwise.

### 9. If we disagree

We talk first, then a mediator we both accept, and only then the courts of Uganda.

### 10. When the tenancy ends

To leave, give us 30 days' notice by phone, SMS or WhatsApp, and we confirm it by message. Your tenancy ends on
the day before a due date, at least 30 days after we get your notice, so your last month is always a whole
month. If you leave without giving notice, you owe the rent until your tenancy would have ended, counted from
the day we learn you have gone, and we take it from your deposit first, if we hold it. We visit the unit when
your tenancy ends, or when we learn you have gone.

- **Notice the landlord gives:** [notice]

Your deposit goes back to you after any rent you still owe and the cost of any damage are taken from it. Rent
you paid ahead for months you did not use goes back to you (clause 4).

### 11. If we stop acting for the landlord

We tell you. Rent you paid ahead for months that start after we stop goes to the landlord, who honors it, unless
your tenancy has ended and clause 4 applies. You get a note saying the date your rent is paid up to. A deposit we
hold goes to the landlord after any rent you still owe is taken from it, and the landlord gives it back to you
when the tenancy ends.

### 12. Changing your due date

To keep our field work well organized, we may move your due date during the tenancy. We give you 30 days' notice,
with the new date and the amount you pay, worked out as clause 13 says, and we update this form. The new date is
then the day written above.

### 13. Part months

To work out a part month, we count every month as 30 days, so each day is one-thirtieth of your monthly rent, and we
round what you pay for the days to the nearest UGX 1,000, with UGX 500 rounding up. When you move in, you pay for the
days from your move-in date to your first due date. When your due date moves, you pay a share of your monthly rent
(pro rata) for the days counted forward from your old due date to the new one. A month that has started is paid in
full: if you leave part-way through it, the rest of it does not go back to you.

The form re-voices the model's plain statements to "we" and "you" and tightens the wording. The meaning is
unchanged, except that the form does not repeat the promise never to lock a tenant out, which the landlord
agreement keeps.

### Not decided in the model

- The late fee, and what the Landlord and Tenant Act allows.
- When the written notice goes out after a tenant misses a due date: with the pause on the next 1st, or at the end
  of the visits.
- For a due-date move: who receives the amount, whether our 15% applies to it, and when it falls due.
- Limits on moving a due date: whether it may move while the tenant owes rent, while a notice is running and while
  rent is paid ahead, how often it may move, and what the updated form must carry.
- Who brings a case for a covered month: the landlord, in the landlord's name (clause 5), or Mwavuli, in its own right
  as the claim for that month passes to it.
- How a tenant gives notice (proposed: phone, SMS or WhatsApp, confirmed by message) and, for a tenant who leaves
  without notice, when the 30 days start to count (proposed: the day we learn the tenant has gone).
- The notice the landlord gives a tenant to end a tenancy.
- Which rent month each payout to the landlord pays, and who funds or recovers the last payout when a tenant leaves.
- When the landlord holds the deposit (a tenant already in the building): who recovers the rent the tenant owes when
  leaving, from rent paid ahead or the landlord from the deposit it holds.
- How soon after a tenancy ends we visit the unit, and the latest day for the refund of rent paid ahead if no visit
  happens.
- Whether the landlord refunds a tenant who leaves after we stop acting for the landlord, for rent paid ahead that we
  paid to the landlord.
- Due days 29 to 31 in months without them, and a tenant who moves in on the due day.
- How electricity, water and garbage shares are worked out for a unit with no meter.
- Who pays the contractor first for damage a tenant causes, and whether our coordination fee is charged to the
  tenant.
- Whether the tenancy form is in English and Luganda and explained aloud, as the landlord agreement is.
