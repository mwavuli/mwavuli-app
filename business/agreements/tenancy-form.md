# Tenancy form

Draft 0.2 for counsel review. Ticket SNT-27. Built from [the business model](../model.md). The drafting notes at
the end say which term each clause takes from. This draft is not legal advice.

The landlord and the tenant sign this form, and Mwavuli Holdings Ltd signs for the landlord. A unit counts as
occupied, and the first-month cover in the [landlord agreement](landlord-agreement.md) starts, once a tenant is
living in it under this form with the rent and deposit written down.

## The tenancy in plain words

In this form "we" means Mwavuli Holdings Ltd, acting for the landlord, and "you" means the tenant.

### 1. Who we are

- This tenancy is between the landlord and you.
- We act for the landlord: we collect the rent and look after the building.

### 2. Paying your rent

- Pay us only, by mobile money or bank. Never cash to our staff.
- Pay to: mobile money number [to fill] or bank account [bank, account name and number].
- Your rent is due on the day written above, each month. The landlord is always paid on the 1st, whatever your
  due date.

### 3. Your deposit

- New tenant: your deposit is paid to us and kept in a separate account. We use it only for what this tenancy
  allows (unpaid rent, damage, legal costs). The rest goes back to you.
- Already living here: the landlord keeps the deposit already held (the amount is written above) and gives it
  back to you.

### 4. Rent paid ahead

- We hold it in a separate account, like deposits.
- We pay it to the landlord month by month on the 1st. Months that have not started stay in that account.
- If you leave early, the months you paid for and did not use go back to you, [to fill: the notice you give and
  any other condition].

### 5. If you do not pay

- If you have not paid by your due date, we remind you, call you and visit you, then send a written notice.
- If it must go to court, the landlord says YES first, and the case is in the landlord's name.
- We never lock you out or remove your belongings.
- Late fee: [to fill: the amount and when it applies]. It goes to us.

### 6. Repairs

- Tell us at [phone or WhatsApp number]. We arrange a trusted contractor.
- Damage you cause can be taken from your deposit (clause 3).

### 7. Electricity, water and garbage

- Your share is charged at cost, with no markup.
- How your share is worked out: [to fill].

### 8. Privacy

- Landlord and tenant details are used only to manage the building. They are not sold or shared otherwise.

### 9. If we disagree

- We talk first.
- Then a mediator we both accept.
- Only then the courts of Uganda.

### 10. When the tenancy ends

- The notice you give and the notice the landlord gives: [to fill].
- Your deposit goes back to you after any rent you still owe and the cost of any damage are taken from it.
- Rent you paid ahead for months you did not use goes back to you (clause 4).

### 11. If we stop acting for the landlord

- We tell you.
- Rent you paid ahead for months after we stop goes to the landlord, who honors it. You get a note: "rent paid
  up to [month]".
- A deposit we hold goes to the landlord after any rent you still owe is taken from it. The landlord gives it
  back to you when the tenancy ends.

The form re-voices the model's plain statements to "we" and "you" and sets them out as short bullets. The
meaning is unchanged.

### Not decided in the model

- Whether a tenant's due date can change during a tenancy, and who may change it.
- The late fee, and what the Landlord and Tenant Act allows.
- The tenant's notice period, and the conditions for refunding unused prepaid rent.
- How electricity, water and garbage shares are worked out.
- Who pays for damage a tenant causes, beyond the deposit.
- Whether the tenancy form is in English and Luganda and explained aloud, as the landlord agreement is.
