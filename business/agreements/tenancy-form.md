# Tenancy form

Draft 0.1 for counsel review. Ticket SNT-27. Built from [the business model](../model.md). The drafting notes at
the end say which term each clause takes from. This draft is not legal advice.

The landlord and the tenant sign this form, and Mwavuli Holdings signs for the landlord. A unit counts as
occupied, and the first-month cover in the [landlord agreement](landlord-agreement.md) starts, once a tenant is
living in it under this form with the rent and deposit written down.

## The tenancy in plain words

In this form "we" means Mwavuli Holdings, acting for the landlord, and "you" means the tenant.

### 1. Who we are

This tenancy is between the landlord and you. We act for the landlord. We collect the rent and look after the
building.

### 2. Paying your rent

Pay your rent to us only, by mobile money or bank, never cash to our staff. Pay to: mobile money number [to
fill] or bank account [bank, account name and number]. Rent is due on the 1st of each month.

### 3. Your deposit

If you are a new tenant, your deposit is paid to us and kept in a separate account. We use it only for what this
tenancy allows (unpaid rent, damage, legal costs), and the rest goes back to you.

If you were already living in the building, the landlord keeps the deposit already held, and the amount is
written above. The landlord gives it back to you.

### 4. Rent paid ahead

If you pay rent ahead, we hold it in a separate account, like deposits, and pay it out to the landlord month by
month on the 1st. Months that have not started stay in that account. If you leave early, the months you paid for
and did not use go back to you under this form, [to fill: the notice you give and any other condition].

### 5. If you do not pay

If you have not paid, we remind you, call you and visit you, and then send you a written notice. If the matter
must go to court, the landlord says YES first, and the case is in the landlord's name. We never lock you out and
never remove your belongings. Late fee: [to fill: the amount and when it applies]. A late fee goes to us.

### 6. Repairs

If something is broken or unsafe, tell us at [phone or WhatsApp number]. We arrange a trusted contractor. If you
cause damage, it can be taken from your deposit (clause 3).

### 7. Electricity, water and garbage

Your share of electricity, water and garbage costs is charged at cost, with no markup. [to fill: how your share
is worked out]

### 8. Privacy

Landlord and tenant details are used only to manage the building and are not sold or shared otherwise.

### 9. If we disagree

We talk first, then a mediator we both accept, and only then the courts of Uganda.

### 10. When the tenancy ends

The notice you give and the notice the landlord gives: [to fill]. When the tenancy ends, your deposit goes back
to you after any rent you still owe and the cost of any damage are taken from it. Rent you paid ahead for months
you did not use goes back to you under clause 4.

### 11. If we stop acting for the landlord

If we stop acting for the landlord, we tell you. Rent you paid ahead for months after we stop goes to the
landlord, who honors it. You get a note that says "rent paid up to [month]". A deposit we hold goes to the
landlord after any rent you still owe is taken from it, and the landlord gives it back to you when the tenancy
ends.

### Not decided in the model

- The rent due date: the 1st is implied by BM1 but not stated.
- The late fee, and what the Landlord and Tenant Act allows.
- The tenant's notice period, and the conditions for refunding unused prepaid rent.
- How electricity, water and garbage shares are worked out.
- Who pays for damage a tenant causes, beyond the deposit.
- Whether the tenancy form is in English and Luganda and explained aloud, as the landlord agreement is.
