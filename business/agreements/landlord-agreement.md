## The agreement in plain words

### 1. We act for you

- We manage your units, collect the rent and arrange the work, as your agent.
- Legal steps are taken in your name, and only with your YES.

### 2. What we do

- We find tenants for your empty rooms.
- We collect the rent.
- We arrange trusted contractors for repairs.
- We look after electricity, water and garbage.
- You get one payment and one statement a month, by SMS or WhatsApp.
- There is no sign-up fee and no letting fee.

### 3. Your building

- We manage homes and business premises in Kampala and its surroundings: rooms, flats, small blocks, shops and
  offices.
- There is no minimum number of units.
- Every unit gets the same terms, whatever its rent: our 15%, the first month covered, and your rent on the 1st.
- Your units are listed in Annex 1.
- **Example:** A landlord with six rooms and one shop gives us all seven units. All seven get the same terms.

### 4. Showing us you own it

- A certificate of title in your name is enough.
- No title? Bring a sale agreement or letters of administration (or probate), a letter from your local council
  (LC1), and your signed statement that you own the building and may let it.
- We always need your national ID.
- Where there is a title, we search it.
- We do not take a building that is in dispute, or that has co-owners who have not all agreed in writing.
- The list is in Annex 2.

### 5. Checking the building

- We walk the building with you first and take photos.
- Unsafe items must be fixed within 30 days, before we start.
- We may decline a building that stays unsafe, that fails the ownership check or the inspection, or that we
  cannot fund. We may also decline a single unit.
- Once we accept the building, the promise applies.
- We write down what we find in the [inspection report](inspection-report.md).

### 6. Your tenants

- A unit is occupied when a tenant lives in it under our [tenancy form](tenancy-form.md), with the rent and
  deposit written down.
- The first-month cover starts on the first 1st after that.
- Tenants already in your building move onto our form after a visit.
- Rent a tenant owes from before you sign up is not covered. We collect it for the same 15%.

### 7. Rent on the 1st

- For every unit with a tenant living in it, you get the rent on the 1st of the month.
- If the tenant has not paid, we still pay you for that month.
- If it is still unpaid by the next 1st, we stop paying for that unit until the tenant pays up or a new tenant
  moves in.
- Each tenant's rent is due on a day written in the tenancy form. The day can differ from tenant to tenant. We
  always pay you on the 1st.
- **Example:** A room rents for UGX 400,000 and the tenant does not pay in March.
  - 1 Mar: you get UGX 340,000 for it (the rent minus our 15%).
  - 1 Apr: the rent is still unpaid, so we send nothing for that room until it is settled.

### 8. How tenants pay, and your statement

- Tenants pay only us, by mobile money or bank. Never cash to our staff.
- You get one statement a month by SMS or WhatsApp, in your chosen language. Paper on request.

### 9. What we charge and how you are paid

- We take 15% of the rent. No rent, no fee.
- On the 1st we send you the rent of every unit with a tenant, minus our 15%. One payment, one statement.
- Costs you have approved are taken off the same payment and listed on the statement.
- A job never takes more than one payout. For a bigger job, you pay the balance before work starts.
- The 15% is charged on rent only: not on deposits, utility or garbage money, or repair costs.
- **Example:** A room rents for UGX 400,000. Our 15% is UGX 60,000. You get UGX 340,000 on the 1st.

### 10. The account your rent is paid from

- We pay your rent from an account we keep for rent payouts and use for nothing else.
- We take on a building only when that account can cover its payouts, so the promise to pay you on the 1st can
  be kept.

### 11. Repairs and building work

- We find a trusted contractor and bring you the price.
- You pay the contractor's price plus our coordination fee of 10%, shown on the quote.
- Above UGX 200,000 on the quote, we wait for your YES before work starts.
- Urgent safety repairs up to UGX 500,000 go ahead first. We tell you the same day.
- Electricity, water and garbage bills are paid at cost, with no markup.
- You say YES by replying to our SMS or WhatsApp message, or on a recorded call that we confirm by text.
- **Example:** A plumber quotes UGX 100,000 to fix a tap.
  - Our 10% is UGX 10,000, so the quote is UGX 110,000.
  - That is below UGX 200,000, so we fix it and list it on your statement.
  - A roof repair at UGX 600,000 plus our UGX 60,000 is UGX 660,000, so we ask you first and you reply YES.

### 12. When a tenant still has not paid

- After the month we covered, we do the chasing: we remind, call and visit, then send a written notice.
- If it must go to court, we ask you first and you say YES.
- We never lock a tenant out. Our time costs you nothing extra.
- A lawyer, the court and a bailiff are paid at cost: from the tenant's deposit first, then by you.
- The month we covered is ours to collect.
- When a tenant pays what is owed, the oldest month is paid first. The month we covered is repaid before later
  months reach you.
- **Example:** A room rents for UGX 400,000 and the tenant misses March.
  - 1 Mar: we pay you UGX 340,000.
  - 1 Apr: still unpaid. We send nothing for that room and give the tenant a written notice.
  - 20 Apr: the tenant pays UGX 400,000. It repays March (UGX 340,000 we advanced and UGX 60,000 fee).
  - 1 May: April is still owed, so we send nothing.
  - When April is paid, we send you UGX 340,000 of it.

### 13. Deposits

- A new tenant's deposit is paid to us and kept in a separate account.
- We use it only for what the tenancy allows (unpaid rent, damage, legal costs). The rest goes back to the
  tenant.
- For tenants already in the building, you keep the deposit you hold. We write the amount down when you sign up,
  and you stay responsible for giving it back.
- **Example:** A new tenant pays a UGX 400,000 deposit to us, and we keep it apart.
  - At move-out, UGX 50,000 is kept for a broken door and UGX 350,000 goes back to the tenant.
  - For a tenant already in the building, you hold UGX 400,000. We note it on the file, and it stays yours to
    return.

### 14. Prepaid rent

- When a tenant pays rent ahead, we hold it in a separate account, like deposits.
- We pay it out month by month on the 1st, minus 15%. Months that have not started stay there.
- If a tenant leaves early, unused months go back to the tenant under the tenancy form, with no fee on them.

### 15. VAT on commercial rent

- VAT a tenant pays on rent is not part of the rent for the 15%.
- We pass it to you in the same payment and show it on the statement.
- Filing it is your job.

### 16. Your duties

- Tell tenants to pay only us, and pass on any rent paid to you the same day.
- Make no side deals on rent or tenants.
- Do not lock a tenant out, and do not cut water or power.
- Let us in with notice.
- Your taxes and rates stay yours.
- Tell us the truth about ownership, debts and disputes.

### 17. Leaving

- You can leave any time with 30 days' written notice, and so can we.
- If we have not paid you by the 3rd, you can leave straight away, with no fee.
- We can end the agreement straight away if you collect rent from tenants yourself, interfere with tenants, or
  refuse a repair that is urgent for safety.
- When it ends, we send you a final statement and pay you within 14 days of the end date.
  - The payment is the rent we collected and have not paid out, plus prepaid rent for months after the end date
    (in full, with no 15%), less costs you approved and any covered month a tenant still owes us.
  - Tenant files go to you.
  - A deposit we hold goes to you after any rent the tenant still owes is taken from it. Tenants are told.
  - Each tenant gets a note, "rent paid up to [month]", and you honor it.
- **Example:** The rent was due to you on 1 May and we have not paid it.
  - On 4 May you tell us in writing that you are leaving.
  - The agreement ends that day, and you owe no fee.

### 18. Privacy

- Landlord and tenant details are used only to manage the building. They are not sold or shared otherwise.

### 19. If we disagree

- We talk first.
- Then a mediator we both accept.
- Only then the courts of Uganda.

### 20. Languages and signing

- The agreement comes in English and Luganda. You sign both.
- One of our officers reads the main points to you out loud, in your language, and answers your questions.
- You sign, or give your thumbprint, in front of a witness you choose.
- A short signed note says who explained it to you and that you understood.
- You keep a copy.

### Not decided in the model

The model does not settle these. The owner and counsel decide them before the agreement is used.

- Who sets the rent for each unit and any increase, and who chooses tenants.
- The tenant's notice period, and the conditions for refunding unused prepaid rent.
- How electricity, water and garbage costs are charged to tenants.
- When rent collected after a covered month is paid to the landlord.
- After the end date, who collects tenants' rent.
- A deposit the landlord holds cannot be used against a covered month: proposed in the model, not approved.
- P5 and BM8: the model's note on P5 says any breach of a landlord duty lets us end the agreement at once, but
  BM8 lists three events.
- A fee for ending on 30 days' notice: none is stated.
- Whether the examples stay in the signed text.
- Whether a tenant's due date can change during a tenancy, and who may change it.
- The late fee in the tenancy form.
