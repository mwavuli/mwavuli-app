## Part 1. The agreement in plain words

### 1. We act for you

We act as your agent for the building. We manage your units, collect the rent and arrange the work. Legal steps
are taken in your name, and only with your YES.

### 2. What we do

We find tenants for your empty rooms, collect the rent, arrange trusted contractors for repairs, and look after
electricity, water and garbage. You get one payment and one statement a month, by SMS or WhatsApp. There is no
sign-up fee and no letting fee.

### 3. Your building

We manage homes and business premises in Kampala and its surroundings: rooms, flats, small blocks, shops and
offices. There is no minimum number of units. A shop or an office gets the same terms as a room: our 15%, the
first month covered, and your rent on the 1st. No unit is left out because its rent is large. Your units are
listed in Annex 1.

For example: A landlord with six rooms and one shop gives us all seven units. All seven get the same terms.

### 4. Showing us you own it

Show us your title, or the papers on our list. We check them before we start. A certificate of title in your
name is enough. If you have no title, bring a sale agreement or letters of administration (or probate), a letter
from your local council (LC1), and your signed statement that you own the building and may let it. We always
need your national ID. Where there is a title, we search it. We do not take a building that is in dispute, or
that has co-owners who have not all agreed in writing. The list is in Annex 2.

### 5. Checking the building

We walk the building with you first and take photos. Unsafe items must be fixed within 30 days, before we start.
We may decline a building that stays unsafe. We may also decline a building or unit we cannot fund, or that
fails the ownership check or the inspection. Once we accept it, the promise applies. We write down what we find
in the [inspection report](inspection-report.md).

### 6. Your tenants

A unit is occupied when a tenant is living in it under our [tenancy form](tenancy-form.md), with the rent and
deposit written down. The first-month cover starts on the first 1st after that. Each tenant who is already in
your building moves onto our form after a visit. Rent a tenant owes from before you sign up is not covered; we
collect it for the same 15%.

### 7. Rent on the 1st

For every unit with a tenant living in it, you get the rent on the 1st of the month. If the tenant has not paid,
we still pay you for that month. If the tenant has still not paid by the next 1st, we stop paying for that unit
until the tenant has paid up or a new tenant moves in.

For example: A room rents for UGX 400,000 and the tenant does not pay in March. On 1 Mar you receive UGX 340,000
for it (the rent minus our 15%). On 1 Apr the rent is still unpaid, so we send nothing for that room until it is
settled.

### 8. How tenants pay, and your statement

Tenants pay only us, by mobile money or bank, never cash to our staff. You get one statement a month by SMS or
WhatsApp in your chosen language, with paper on request.

### 9. What we charge and how you are paid

We take 15% of the rent. No rent, no fee. On the 1st we send you the rent of every unit with a tenant, minus our
15%, in one payment with one statement. Costs you have approved are taken off the same payment and listed on the
statement. A job never takes more than one payout: for a bigger job, you pay the balance before work starts. The
15% is charged on rent only: not on deposits, not on utility or garbage money and not on repair costs.

For example: A room rents for UGX 400,000. Our 15% is UGX 60,000. You receive UGX 340,000 on the 1st.

### 10. The account your rent is paid from

We pay your rent from an account we keep for rent payouts and use for nothing else. We take on a building only
when that account can cover its payouts, so the promise to pay you on the 1st can be kept.

### 11. Repairs and building work

When something needs fixing or building, we find a trusted contractor and bring you the price. You pay the
contractor's price plus our coordination fee of 10%, shown on the quote. Above UGX 200,000 on the quote we wait
for your YES before work starts. Urgent safety repairs up to UGX 500,000 go ahead first, and we tell you the
same day. Electricity, water and garbage bills are paid at cost, with no markup. You say YES by replying to our
SMS or WhatsApp message, or on a recorded call that we confirm by text.

For example: A plumber quotes UGX 100,000 to fix a tap. Our 10% is UGX 10,000, so the quote is UGX 110,000. That
is below UGX 200,000, so we fix it and list it on your statement. A roof repair at UGX 600,000 plus our UGX
60,000 is UGX 660,000, so we ask you first and you reply YES.

### 12. When a tenant still has not paid

If a tenant still has not paid after the month we covered, we do the chasing for you. We remind, call and visit,
then send a written notice. If it must go to court, we ask you first and you say YES. We never lock a tenant
out. Our time costs you nothing extra. A lawyer, the court and a bailiff are paid at cost: from the tenant's
deposit first, then by you. The month we covered is ours to collect. When a tenant pays what is owed, the oldest
month is paid first, so the month we covered is repaid before later months reach you.

For example: A room rents for UGX 400,000. The tenant does not pay in March, so we pay you UGX 340,000 on 1 Mar.
On 1 Apr it is still unpaid: we send nothing for that room and give the tenant a written notice. On 20 Apr the
tenant pays UGX 400,000. It repays March, so we keep it (UGX 340,000 we advanced and UGX 60,000 fee). April is
still owed, so we still send nothing on 1 May. When April is paid, we send you UGX 340,000 of it.

### 13. Deposits

A new tenant's deposit is paid to us and kept in a separate account. We use it only for what the tenancy allows
(unpaid rent, damage, legal costs), and the rest goes back to the tenant. For tenants already in the building,
you keep the deposit you hold. We write the amount down when you sign up, and you stay responsible for giving it
back.

For example: A new tenant pays a UGX 400,000 deposit to us and we keep it apart. At move-out UGX 50,000 is kept
for a broken door and UGX 350,000 goes back to the tenant. For a tenant already in the building, you hold UGX
400,000. We note it on the file and it stays yours to return.

### 14. Prepaid rent

When a tenant pays rent ahead, we hold it in a separate account, like deposits, and pay it out month by month on
the 1st, minus 15%. Months that haven't started stay there. If a tenant leaves early, unused months go back to
the tenant under the tenancy form, with no fee on them.

### 15. VAT on commercial rent

VAT a tenant pays on rent is not part of the rent for the 15%. We pass it to you in the same payment and show it
on the statement. Filing it is your job.

### 16. Your duties

- Tell tenants to pay only us, and pass on any rent paid to you the same day.
- Make no side deals on rent or tenants.
- Do not lock a tenant out, and do not cut water or power.
- Let us in with notice.
- Your taxes and rates stay yours.
- Tell us the truth about ownership, debts and disputes.

### 17. Leaving

You can leave any time with 30 days' written notice, and so can we. If we have not paid you by the 3rd, you can
leave straight away, with no fee. We can end the agreement straight away if you collect rent from tenants
yourself, interfere with tenants, or refuse a repair that is urgent for safety.

When the agreement ends, we send you a final statement and pay you within 14 days of the end date. It pays the
rent we collected and haven't paid out, plus prepaid rent for months after the end date in full with no 15%,
less costs you approved and any covered month a tenant still owes us. Tenant files go to you. A deposit we hold
goes to you after any rent the tenant still owes is taken from it, and tenants are told. Each tenant gets a
note, "rent paid up to [month]", and you honor it.

For example: The rent was due to you on 1 May and we have not paid it. On 4 May you tell us in writing that you
are leaving. The agreement ends that day and you owe no fee.

### 18. Privacy

Landlord and tenant details are used only to manage the building and are not sold or shared otherwise.

### 19. If we disagree

We talk first, then a mediator we both accept, and only then the courts of Uganda.

### 20. Languages and signing

The agreement comes in English and Luganda, and you sign both. One of our officers reads the main points to you
out loud, in your language, and answers your questions. You sign, or give your thumbprint, in front of a witness
you choose. A short signed note says who explained it to you and that you understood. You keep a copy.

## Part 2. The full terms

Part 2 says the same things as Part 1 in numbered paragraphs, with the words defined.

### S1. Words used

- **1.1** "We", "us" and "our" mean Mwavuli Holdings. "You" and "your" mean the landlord named at the top. If
  there is more than one owner, "you" means all of them together.
- **1.2** "The building" is the building named at the top. A "unit" is a room, flat, shop or office in it that
  is listed in Annex 1.
- **1.3** A unit is "occupied" when a tenant is living in it under our tenancy form, with the rent and deposit
  written down.
- **1.4** "Rent" is the monthly rent a tenant owes for a unit. It does not include a deposit, money for
  electricity, water or garbage, VAT, a late fee, costs we pass through, or our coordination fee.
- **1.5** "Our fee" is 15% of the rent. "Net rent" is the rent minus our fee.
- **1.6** The "payout date" is the 1st of each month.
- **1.7** The "covered month" of a unit is the first month of unpaid rent that we pay you on the payout date, as
  set out in S3.
- **1.8** "Arrears" is rent that a tenant owes and has not paid.
- **1.9** The "payout account" is the account in clause 10 of Part 1.
- **1.10** A "quote" is the price for a job, shown to you in writing, with our coordination fee on it.
- **1.11** "YES" is your reply by SMS or WhatsApp, or on a recorded call that we confirm by text.
- **1.12** The "end date" is the day this agreement ends.

### S2. How we pay you

- **2.1** On each payout date we pay you the net rent of every occupied unit, in one payment with one statement.
- **2.2** We take off that payment the costs you have approved, and we list them on the statement.
- **2.3** A job never takes more than one payout. For a bigger job, you pay the balance before work starts.
- **2.4** We charge our fee on rent only. We do not charge it on deposits, on money for utilities or garbage, or
  on repair costs.
- **2.5** We charge no sign-up fee, no letting fee and no yearly fee, and we charge none to tenants. Late fees
  written in the tenancy form go to us.
- **2.6** We pay you at the account or mobile money number in Annex 4.

### S3. The covered month

- **3.1** If the tenant of an occupied unit has not paid the rent for a month, we still pay you the net rent for
  that month on the payout date. That month is the covered month of that unit.
- **3.2** A unit has one covered month at a time. If the covered month is still unpaid on the next payout date,
  we do not pay you for that unit until the arrears are cleared or a new tenant moves in. When the arrears are
  cleared, the unit can have a new covered month.
- **3.3** When we pay a covered month, the claim for that rent passes to us, and we may collect it from the
  tenant in our own right.
- **3.4** We earn our fee on a covered month only when that rent is collected.
- **3.5** When a tenant pays what is owed, the oldest month is paid first. The covered month is repaid to us
  first. Later months are paid to you, minus our fee.

### S4. When a tenant has not paid after the covered month

- **4.1** We remind, call and visit the tenant, and then send a written notice.
- **4.2** If the matter must go to court, we ask you first. We take legal steps only in your name and only with
  your YES.
- **4.3** We never lock a tenant out and never remove a tenant's belongings.
- **4.4** Our own time costs you nothing extra. A lawyer, the court and a bailiff are paid at cost. They are
  paid from the tenant's deposit first, if we hold it. Where you hold the deposit, or it is not enough, you pay
  them.

### S5. Money we hold for others

- **5.1** A new tenant's deposit is paid to us and kept in a separate account. We use it only for what the
  tenancy allows (unpaid rent, damage, legal costs), and the rest goes back to the tenant.
- **5.2** For a tenant who was in the building before you signed up, you keep the deposit you hold. We write the
  amount in Annex 3, and you stay responsible for giving it back.
- **5.3** Rent paid ahead for months that have not started is held in a separate account, like deposits. We pay
  it out month by month on the payout date, minus our fee. If a tenant leaves early, the unused months go back
  to the tenant under the tenancy form, with no fee on them.
- **5.4** We pay your rent from the payout account, which we keep for rent payouts and use for nothing else. We
  take on a building only when the payout account can cover its payouts.

### S6. Repairs, utilities and garbage

- **6.1** We arrange the work as your agent, and the contractor does it.
- **6.2** You pay the contractor's price plus our coordination fee of 10%. The fee is shown on the quote.
- **6.3** We wait for your YES before work starts on any job above UGX 200,000 on the quote.
- **6.4** We may start an urgent safety repair up to UGX 500,000 without your YES. We tell you the same day.
- **6.5** Electricity, water and garbage bills are paid at cost, with no markup.

### S7. Taking on the building

- **7.1** Before we start, you show us proof of ownership as listed in Annex 2. We check it, and where there is
  a title we search it.
- **7.2** We do not take a building that is in dispute, or that has co-owners who have not all agreed in
  writing.
- **7.3** We walk the building with you and take photos. Unsafe items must be fixed within 30 days, before we
  start.
- **7.4** We may decline a building or unit that we cannot fund, that fails the ownership check or the
  inspection, or that stays unsafe. Once we accept the building, the promise in this agreement applies.
- **7.5** Each tenant already in the building moves onto our tenancy form after a visit. Rent a tenant owes from
  before you sign up is not covered. We collect it for the same fee.

### S8. Your duties

- **8.1** You tell tenants to pay only us, and you pass on any rent paid to you the same day.
- **8.2** You make no side deals with tenants on rent or on who lives in a unit.
- **8.3** You do not lock a tenant out, and you do not cut a tenant's water or power.
- **8.4** You let us in with notice.
- **8.5** Your taxes and rates stay yours.
- **8.6** You tell us the truth about ownership, debts and disputes.

### S9. Ending the agreement

- **9.1** Either of us can end this agreement with 30 days' written notice.
- **9.2** If we have not paid you by the end of the 3rd of a month, you can end this agreement at once, with no
  fee. We have not paid you when the money has not reached your account or mobile money number in Annex 4.
- **9.3** We can end this agreement at once if you collect rent from tenants yourself, interfere with tenants,
  or refuse a repair that is urgent for safety.
- **9.4** Within 14 days of the end date we send you a final statement and pay you. The payment is the rent we
  collected and have not paid out, plus prepaid rent for months after the end date, in full and with no fee,
  less costs you approved, and less any covered month a tenant still owes us.
- **9.5** Tenant files go to you. A deposit we hold goes to you after any rent the tenant still owes is taken
  from it. We tell the tenants.
- **9.6** We give each tenant with prepaid rent a note that says "rent paid up to [month]", and you honor it.

### S10. VAT, privacy and disagreements

- **10.1** VAT a tenant pays on rent is not part of the rent for our fee. We pass it to you in the same payment
  and show it on the statement. Filing it is your job.
- **10.2** Landlord and tenant details are used only to manage the building. We do not sell them or share them
  otherwise.
- **10.3** If we disagree, we talk first. If that does not settle it, we use a mediator we both accept. Only
  then do we go to the courts of Uganda.

### S11. Languages and signing

- **11.1** This agreement comes in English and Luganda. You sign both.
- **11.2** An officer of ours reads the main points to you out loud, in your language, and answers your
  questions.
- **11.3** You sign, or give your thumbprint, in front of a witness you choose.
- **11.4** A short signed note, the explanation note, says who explained the agreement to you and that you
  understood.
- **11.5** You keep a copy.

### S12. The building and the units

- **12.1** We manage homes and business premises in Kampala and its surroundings. There is no minimum number of
  units.
- **12.2** Every unit has the same terms, whatever its rent. A shop or an office is not left out because its
  rent is large.
- **12.3** The figures in this agreement are in clause 21 of Part 1.
