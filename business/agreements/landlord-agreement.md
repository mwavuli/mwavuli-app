## The agreement in plain words

### 1. We act for you

We act as your agent: we manage your units, collect the rent and arrange the work. Legal steps are taken in your
name, and only with your YES.

### 2. What we do

We find tenants for your empty rooms, collect the rent, arrange trusted contractors for repairs, and look after
electricity, water and garbage. You get one payment and one statement a month, by SMS or WhatsApp. There is no
sign-up fee and no letting fee.

### 3. Your building

We manage homes and business premises in Kampala and its surroundings: rooms, flats, small blocks, shops and
offices, with no minimum number of units. Every unit we take on gets the same terms, whatever its rent: we take
15% of its rent, we cover its first missed month, and we pay you on the 1st. Your units are listed in Annex 1.

### 4. Showing us you own it

Show us your title, or the papers on our list, and we check them before we start. A certificate of title in your
name is enough. With no title, bring a sale agreement or letters of administration (or probate), a letter from
your local council (LC1), and your signed statement that you own the building and may let it. We always need
your national ID. We search the title where there is one, and we do not take a building in dispute or whose
co-owners have not all agreed in writing. The list is in Annex 2.

### 5. Checking the building

We walk the building with you first and take photos. Unsafe items must be fixed within 30 days, before we start.
We may decline a building or unit that stays unsafe, fails the ownership check or the inspection, or that we
cannot fund. Once we accept the building, the promise applies. What we find goes in the [inspection
report](inspection-report.md).

### 6. Your tenants

A unit is occupied when a tenant lives in it under our [tenancy form](tenancy-form.md), with the rent and
deposit written down. The first-month cover starts on the first 1st after that. A new tenant also pays, at move-in,
for the days from move-in to the first due date, and we pay you that rent, minus our 15%, with your first payout
after move-in. Tenants already in your building move onto our form after a visit. Rent owed from before you sign up
is not covered; we collect it for the same 15%.

A tenant's rent month runs from one due date to the next. A tenant on our form leaves by giving us 30 days' notice.
The tenancy ends on the day before a due date, at least 30 days after we get the notice, so the last rent month is
always whole. A tenant who leaves without the notice owes the rent until it would have ended, and we take it from
the deposit first, if we hold it. We visit the unit when the tenancy ends, or when we learn the tenant has gone.

### 7. Rent on the 1st

For every unit with a tenant living in it, you get the rent on the 1st of the month. If the tenant has not paid,
we still pay you for that month. If it is still unpaid by the next 1st, we stop paying for that unit until the
tenant pays up or a new tenant moves in. Each tenant's rent is due on a day set in the tenancy form, but we
always pay you on the 1st. Each payout pays the rent month that starts that month, and we do not pay a rent month
that starts after a tenancy has ended.

For example: a room rents for UGX 400,000 and the tenant does not pay in March. On 1st March you get UGX 340,000
for it (the rent minus our 15%). On 1st April it is still unpaid, so we send nothing for that room until it is
settled.

To keep our field work well organized, we may move a tenant's due date during the tenancy. We give the tenant
30 days' notice and update the tenancy form. The tenant pays a share of the monthly rent for the days counted
forward from the old due date to the new one (pro rata). Your payout stays on the 1st.

For example: we move a tenant's due date from the 5th to the 20th, starting in April. The tenant pays UGX 400,000 a
month, and the 15 days from the 5th to the 20th of April are charged pro rata: half a month, UGX 200,000. You are
paid on 1st April as always. Moving a date from the 25th to the 5th counts 10 days, which on that rent is
UGX 133,000.

### 8. How tenants pay, and your statement

Tenants pay only us, by mobile money or bank, never cash to our staff. You get one statement a month by SMS or
WhatsApp, in your chosen language, with paper on request.

### 9. What we charge and how you are paid

We take 15% of the rent. No rent, no fee. On the 1st we send you the rent of every unit with a tenant, minus our
15%, in one payment with one statement. Costs you have approved come off the same payment and are listed on the
statement. A job never takes more than one payout: for a bigger job, you pay the balance before work starts. The
15% is charged on rent only, not on deposits, utility or garbage money, or repair costs.

For example: a room rents for UGX 400,000. Our 15% is UGX 60,000, so you get UGX 340,000 on the 1st.

### 10. Repairs and building work

We find a trusted contractor and bring you the price. You pay the contractor's price plus our coordination fee
of 10%, shown on the quote. Above UGX 200,000 on the quote, we wait for your YES before work starts. You say YES
by replying to our SMS or WhatsApp message, or on a recorded call that we confirm by text. Urgent safety repairs
up to UGX 500,000 go ahead first, and we tell you the same day. A tenant pays for damage the tenant causes, in
full, once the repairs are done. If the tenant does not, it is taken from the tenant's deposit. Electricity,
water and garbage bills are paid at cost, with no markup.

For example: a UGX 100,000 tap repair plus our 10% is UGX 110,000, so we just fix it and list it on your
statement. A roof repair at UGX 660,000 (UGX 600,000 plus our UGX 60,000) needs your YES first.

### 11. When a tenant has not paid

If a tenant misses the due date, we do the chasing from that day: we remind, call and visit, then send a written
notice. Should the matter need to go to court, the case is brought in your name, and only with your YES. We never
lock a tenant out, and our time costs you nothing extra. A lawyer, the court and a bailiff are paid at cost, from
the tenant's deposit first and then by you. The month we covered is ours to collect. When a tenant pays what is
owed, the oldest month is paid first, so the month we covered is repaid before later months reach you.

For example: a room rents for UGX 400,000, due on the 5th, and the tenant misses 5th March. We pay you UGX 340,000
on 1st March, start chasing on 5th March, and send nothing for that room on 1st April. If the tenant then pays
UGX 400,000, it repays March to us, so April is still owed. When April is paid, we send you UGX 340,000 of it.

### 12. Deposits

A new tenant's deposit is paid to us and kept in a separate account. We use it only for what the tenancy allows
(unpaid rent, damage, legal costs), and the rest goes back to the tenant. For tenants already in the building,
you keep the deposit you hold: we write the amount down when you sign up, and you stay responsible for giving it
back.

For example: a new tenant pays a UGX 400,000 deposit to us. At move-out UGX 50,000 is kept for a broken door and
UGX 350,000 goes back to the tenant.

### 13. Prepaid rent

Rent a tenant pays ahead is held in a separate account, like deposits, and paid out month by month on the 1st,
minus 15%. When a tenant leaves, the rent held for rent months that start after the tenancy ends goes back to the
tenant, with no fee on it, within 14 days of our visit to the unit. If we hold the deposit, we first take what the
tenant still owes from there, and then from this rent. A rent month that has started is not refunded.

### 14. VAT on commercial rent

VAT a tenant pays on rent is not part of the rent for the 15%. We pass it to you in the same payment and show it
on the statement. Filing it is your job.

### 15. Your duties

- Tell tenants to pay only us, and pass on any rent paid to you the same day.
- Make no side deals on rent or tenants.
- Do not lock a tenant out, and do not cut water or power.
- Let us in with notice.
- Your taxes and rates stay yours.
- Tell us the truth about ownership, debts and disputes.

### 16. Leaving

You can leave any time with 30 days' written notice, and so can we. If we have not paid you by the 3rd, you can
leave straight away, with no fee. We can end the agreement straight away if you collect rent from tenants
yourself, interfere with tenants, or refuse a repair that is urgent for safety.

When the agreement ends, we send you a final statement and pay you within 14 days of the end date: the rent we
collected and have not paid out, plus prepaid rent for rent months that start after the end date, in full with no
15%, except rent that goes back to a tenant whose tenancy has ended, less costs you approved and any covered month a
tenant still owes us. Tenant files go to you. A deposit we hold goes to you after any rent the tenant still owes is
taken from it, and tenants are told. Each tenant gets a note saying the date their rent is paid up to, and you
honor it.

### 17. Privacy

Landlord and tenant details are used only to manage the building and are not sold or shared otherwise.

### 18. If we disagree

We talk first, then a mediator we both accept, and only then the courts of Uganda.

### 19. Languages and signing

The agreement comes in English and Luganda. You choose one, and you and we sign that version. One of our
officers sits down with you, explains the agreement in your language and answers your questions. You sign, or
give your thumbprint, in front of a witness you choose. You keep a copy.

### Not decided in the model

The model does not settle these. The owner and counsel decide them before the agreement is used.

- Who sets the rent for each unit and any increase, and who chooses tenants.
- How electricity, water and garbage costs are charged to tenants.
- Who pays the contractor first for damage a tenant causes, and whether our coordination fee is charged to the
  tenant.
- When rent collected after a covered month is paid to the landlord.
- After the end date, who collects tenants' rent.
- A deposit the landlord holds cannot be used against a covered month: proposed in the model, not approved.
- P5 and BM8: the model's note on P5 says any breach of a landlord duty lets us end the agreement at once, but
  BM8 lists three events.
- A fee for ending on 30 days' notice: none is stated.
- Whether the examples stay in the signed text.
- When the written notice goes out after a tenant misses a due date: with the pause on the next 1st, or at the end
  of the visits.
- For a due-date move: who receives the pro rata amount, whether our 15% applies to it, and when it falls due.
- Limits on moving a due date: whether it may move while the tenant owes rent, while a notice is running and while
  rent is paid ahead, how often it may move, from when the new date applies, and what the updated tenancy form must
  carry.
- Who brings a case for a covered month: you, in your name and with your YES, or we, in our own right, since the
  claim for that month passes to us (clause 11).
- How a tenant gives notice (by phone, SMS or WhatsApp, confirmed by message) and, for a tenant who leaves without
  notice, counting the 30 days from the day we learn the tenant has gone: proposed in the model, not approved.
- When we do not hold a tenant's deposit (a tenant already in your building): who recovers the rent the tenant owes
  when leaving, from rent paid ahead or you from the deposit you hold.
- How soon after a tenancy ends we visit the unit, and the latest day for the refund of rent paid ahead if no visit
  happens.
- Whether you refund a tenant who leaves after the agreement ends, for rent paid ahead that we paid to you.
- The notice a landlord gives a tenant to end a tenancy.
- Due days 29 to 31 in months without them, and a tenant who moves in on the due day.
- The late fee in the tenancy form.
