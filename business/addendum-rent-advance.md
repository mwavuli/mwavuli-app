# Addendum: rent advance

Status: version 0.1, scoped for later. Priority: low. Follow-up work is ticket SNT-26.

An opt-in advance on a landlord's future rent. It is not part of the initial launch offer, pitch or agreements, and it
is kept apart from the [business model](model.md). It is added only after counsel confirms the license position, the
open terms below are settled and a separate advance agreement is written and reviewed. IDs and source tags follow the
model.

## Terms

### BM13 · The advance

*Source: Owner. Stated on 2026-11-10.*

**Stated terms**

- It is opt-in: a landlord asks for it, and it is never added to an agreement by default.
- Mwavuli can advance up to 3 to 6 months of the landlord's future net rent collections.
- The cost is 1.5% a month on the reducing balance, about 19.6% a year.
- The advance is not covered by the 1-month guarantee cap (BM1 in the model). It is repaid whatever the tenants do.
- The rent payments are the security. The whole balance is repaid once Mwavuli is no longer the landlord's agent.
- Mwavuli lends its own funds, under its own license (assumed).

### BM14 · Where it sits

*Source: Owner*

The advance is a separate addendum with low priority. It is not in the initial launch offer, the launch pitch or the
launch agreements.

### BM15 · Repayment

*Source: Owner. The reading of "not covered by the cap" was proposed and accepted.*

**Plain statement (draft)** You get up to 3 to 6 months of your rent now. We keep your next payments until it
is paid back, plus 1.5% a month on what you still owe. While you owe us, we do not cover a month a tenant
misses on that rent: the debt stays and the 1.5% keeps running. If you stop using us as your agent, you pay
back what you still owe at once.

**Example** 10 rooms rent for UGX 400,000, so the net payout is UGX 3,400,000 a month.

| Advance (UGX)        | Payouts kept | Interest (UGX) | Total repaid (UGX) | Returned (UGX) |
|----------------------|--------------|----------------|--------------------|----------------|
| 3 months: 10,200,000 | 4            | 316,836        | 10,516,836         | 3,083,164      |
| 6 months: 20,400,000 | 7            | 1,142,656      | 21,542,656         | 2,257,344      |

Returned is the part of the last payout the landlord gets back. 1.5% a month on the reducing balance is about 19.6%
a year.

**Why** The landlord gets cash now against rent that is already coming. Keeping the payouts is the simplest rule to
explain and the safest for Mwavuli, and the repayment is cheaper for the landlord than a fixed term.

## Open

Parked until the addendum is picked up:

- Who can opt in, and how the 3 to 6 month limit is set.
- What happens on exit in each case, including when Mwavuli ends the agreement or has not paid by the 3rd.
- What the landlord is told and signs: a separate agreement in English and Luganda, with its own explanation (BM11 in
  the model) and the total repayable in UGX.
- How much money Mwavuli sets aside for advances, apart from the float that pays the 1st.
- Other charges. None is stated; the plain rule would be "1.5% a month and no other fee".
- What the money may be used for, and whether a spouse or co-owner must consent.

## Counsel note

- This is lending in substance, whatever it is called: money advanced, interest charged, repayment due whatever the
  tenants do. Lending money at interest as a business likely needs a money-lender license in Uganda (the Tier 4
  Microfinance Institutions and Money Lenders Act, 2016, regulated by the Uganda Microfinance Regulatory Authority).
  Confirm before any advance is offered or advertised, and confirm the interest cap and the disclosure rules.
- Security over future rent: confirm how to take and enforce an assignment of rents, what tenants must be told, and
  what happens when the building is co-owned or inherited.
- "Repaid in full when the agent ends" sits beside BM8 in the model (leave any time, no fee). Draft it so BM8 stays
  true: no exit fee, and any balance still owing is repaid.
- The landlords are the audience described in BM3 in the model. The advance agreement states the total repayable in
  UGX and is explained aloud before signing.
- A seventh service changes the six on the business card and the brand icon set (brand D15, I7). Log it for the
  brand follow-up when the addendum is picked up.
