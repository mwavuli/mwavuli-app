# Mwavuli business model

Status: version 0.6, for owner sign-off and counsel review. Ticket SNT-25. Interview started on 2026-11-10.
Interview transcript: [grill-me session](../.scratch/grill-me/1794346863-mwavuli-business-model.md).

What Mwavuli Property Management offers a landlord, what it charges and what each side does. It is the single
source for the landlord agreements and for the pitch to prospective landlords. It is not legal advice: counsel
reviews every term before an agreement is used.

## How to read

Each term has an ID (BM1, BM2), a source, a **Plain statement**, an **Example**, a **Why** and a **Counsel note**.

- The **Plain statement** is the wording a landlord hears and reads. The agreements and the pitch take it as
  written.
- The **Counsel note** is for the lawyer and the accountant. It is not landlord-facing text.

| Source   | Meaning                                                  |
|----------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Owner    | Decided by the owner during the SNT-25 interview         |
| Proposed | Added to complete the model; open until the owner agrees |

Examples use round, illustrative figures in UGX. They show how a rule works; they are not quotes.

In the plain statements, "Mwavuli" and "we" mean the contracting company (see "The company").

## The offer in brief

### BM18 · The landlord pitch

*Source: Owner. Drafted from the terms below and approved by the owner.*

**Say aloud**

1. We pay you on the 1st, for every room with a tenant.
2. We take 15% of the rent. No rent, no fee.
3. Leave any time with 30 days' notice.

**Paragraph** Give us your building and we pay you the rent on the 1st of every month, for every room with a
tenant, minus our 15%. If a tenant doesn't pay, we cover that first month and do the chasing, so you are not
left waiting. We find tenants for your empty rooms, collect the rent, arrange trusted contractors for repairs,
and look after electricity, water and garbage. You get one payment and one statement a month, by SMS or
WhatsApp. We ask you before any repair above UGX 200,000. There is no sign-up fee and no letting fee. If it
isn't working, leave with 30 days' notice.

**Worked example** 10 rooms at UGX 400,000, 9 with tenants. Your statement for 1st April:

| Item                                          | UGX       |
|-----------------------------------------------|-----------|
| Rent from 9 rooms with tenants                | 3,600,000 |
| Our 15%                                       | -540,000  |
| Repair: tap, plumber UGX 100,000 plus our 10% | -110,000  |
| Paid to you on the 1st                        | 2,950,000 |

The empty room earns no rent and costs no fee, and we look for a tenant. If a tenant misses March, we still pay
you for that room on 1st March (UGX 340,000), and we start chasing on the day the tenant's rent was due. If it is
still unpaid on 1st April, we pause that room until it is settled, we keep chasing, and if it goes to court you say
YES first.

**Rules for using it**

- Say "we cover the first month", never "always paid". Use "guaranteed" only next to "we cover the first month".
- Quote the figures as written. The three lines hold the only numbers a landlord needs to remember: the 1st, 15%
  and 30 days.
- Every claim traces to a term: BM1, BM2, BM4, BM5, BM8, BM11, BM17, P1 and P7.

**Counsel note**

- The pitch is an advertisement. Counsel checks it against the agreement before it is printed, sent or recorded.
- The Luganda text is a complete translation, reviewed by a qualified person (BM11).

## Audience

### BM3 · Who the offer is written for

*Source: Owner*

The main customer is a landlord in Uganda with little formal education, little knowledge of the law and little
patience for legal language.

**Rules**

- Every plain statement passes the read-aloud test: a staff member can say it to a landlord in one breath and the
  landlord can say it back.
- Every term carries an example in UGX with round figures. The short mechanics in BM12 are single plain
  statements.
- The offer carries few numbers and they are round, so a landlord can remember all of them.
- Landlord documents are short. The full legal terms sit in a schedule behind them, and the plain statements say
  what they mean.
- Counsel notes stay out of landlord-facing text.

**Why** A landlord who cannot follow a term cannot give informed consent to it, and an agreement the landlord
did not understand is the first one to be disputed. Plain terms are also what make the pitch believable.

**Counsel note**

- The Illiterates Protection Act may require a signed explanation when a document is given to someone who cannot
  read the language it is written in. Confirm what it requires and how to meet it at signing.
- Ambiguous wording is usually read against the party that drafted it, which is Mwavuli. Plain drafting protects
  Mwavuli too.

## The offer

### BM1 · Rent on the 1st

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** For every unit with a tenant living in it, you get the rent on the 1st of the month. If the
tenant has not paid, we still pay you for that month. If the tenant has still not paid by the next 1st, we stop
paying for that unit until the tenant has paid up or a new tenant moves in.

**Example** A room rents for UGX 400,000 and the tenant does not pay in March. On 1st March you receive
UGX 340,000 for it (the rent minus our 15%). On 1st April the rent is still unpaid, so we send nothing for that
room until it is settled.

**Why** A cap turns an open-ended liability into one month per unit that Mwavuli can fund and a lawyer can write,
and the promise stays true for every month inside it.

**Counsel note**

- The cap is per unit and resets when the arrears are cleared.
- When Mwavuli pays a month the tenant has not paid, the claim for that rent passes to Mwavuli. Draft it so
  Mwavuli can collect that month from the tenant. Who brings a case for it is open (BM5).
- A paid guarantee of rent may count as insurance or suretyship. Confirm whether it needs a license or an
  approval and how to word it.
- "Occupied" is defined in P2.

### BM20 · Rent due dates

*Source: Owner. That Mwavuli may move a due date during a tenancy, and how the days are counted, were decided on
2026-11-13.*

**Plain statement** Each tenant's rent is due on a day of the month written in the tenancy form. The day can differ
from tenant to tenant. To keep our field work well organized, we may move a tenant's due date during the tenancy.
We give the tenant 30 days' notice and update the tenancy form. The tenant pays a share of the monthly rent for the
days counted forward from the old due date to the new one (pro rata). We always pay you on the 1st.

**Example** One tenant's rent is due on the 5th and another's on the 20th. On 1st March we pay you for both rooms.
If the tenant due on the 20th has not paid by 20th March, March is that room's covered month. The tenant due on the
5th pays UGX 400,000 a month. To keep our field work well organized, we move that due date to the 20th: we give the
tenant 30 days' notice and update the tenancy form, and from April the rent is due on the 20th. The 15 days from
the 5th to the 20th of April are charged pro rata: half a month, UGX 200,000. We pay you on 1st April as always.

**Why** Tenants move in and are paid on different days. Mwavuli plans its field work around due dates, so it moves a
tenant's due date where that helps the work run well, and the tenant pays pro rata for the days from the old date to
the new one. The payout date stays on the 1st, so the landlord's day never moves.

**Rule** A due-date move is a part month, counted as P14 says. The days are counted forward from the old due date to
the new one: the 5th to the 20th is 15 days, the 20th to the 5th is 15 days, and the 25th to the 5th is 10 days. The
amount for the days is rounded to the nearest UGX 1,000, a half rounding up: on UGX 400,000, the 25th to the 5th is
10 days, UGX 133,333, charged as UGX 133,000.

**Counsel note**

- The covered month and the chasing start when a tenant misses its due date (BM5). The test that stops the cover
  still runs on payout dates: if the covered month is unpaid on the next 1st, we stop paying for that unit (BM1).
- A later due date means Mwavuli carries that tenant's rent for longer. The payout account test (BM17) already
  assumes that no rent has come in.
- Mwavuli moves a due date on its own initiative, with 30 days' notice and the tenancy form updated (owner,
  2026-11-13). The landlord is not informed. The landlord-facing texts say that we may move a due date, but not that
  the landlord is not told, so confirm that the landlord's signature on the agreement is enough authority for
  Mwavuli to move a due date without asking or telling the landlord.
- Confirm that the tenancy form the tenant signs lets Mwavuli move a due date on 30 days' notice without the tenant's
  agreement, how the notice is given and recorded, and what the updated form must carry.
- The counting of the days is P14's. Its counsel note has the questions.
- Confirm whether a due date may move while the tenant owes rent, while a notice is running and while rent is paid
  ahead, since a later date could undo a due date the tenant has already missed, restart the chasing (BM5) or leave a
  notice ending on a day that is no longer the day before a due date, and how often a due date may move. Open.
- For the accountant: how a pro rata amount is recorded and held. Who receives it, whether the 15% applies to it and
  when it falls due are open; the owner's rule is only that the payout stays on the 1st.

### BM4 · What we charge and how you are paid

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** We take 15% of the rent. No rent, no fee. On the 1st we send you the rent of every unit with a
tenant, minus our 15%, in one payment with one statement. Costs you have approved are taken off the same payment
and listed on the statement. A job never takes more than one payout: for a bigger job, you pay the balance before
work starts. The 15% is charged on rent only: not on deposits, not on utility or garbage money and not on repair
costs.

**Example** A room rents for UGX 400,000. Our 15% is UGX 60,000. You receive UGX 340,000 on the 1st.

**Why** One payment and one statement mean a landlord never has a bill to pay and can check the month at a
glance.

**Counsel note**

- Fee base: rent only, excluding deposits, pass-through costs and the works coordination fee.
- In a covered month (BM1) Mwavuli pays out 85% of the rent and earns its 15% only if that rent is collected
  later.
- For the accountant: VAT on the fee, withholding tax on the fee, and whether the pitch can call 15% the final
  price.
- A job never takes more than one payout, so the 1st payout stays whole and Mwavuli does not lend. Confirm how the
  landlord pays the balance (to the contractor or to Mwavuli) and how it is recorded.

### BM2 · Repairs and building work

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** When something needs fixing or building, we find a trusted contractor and bring you the price.
You pay the contractor's price plus our coordination fee of 10%, shown on the quote. Above UGX 200,000 on the quote
we wait for your YES before work starts. A tenant pays for damage the tenant causes, in full, once the repairs are
done. If the tenant does not pay, it is taken from the tenant's deposit. Electricity, water and garbage bills are
paid at cost, with no markup.

**Example** A plumber quotes UGX 100,000 to fix a tap. Our 10% is UGX 10,000, so the quote is UGX 110,000. That is
below UGX 200,000, so we fix it and list it on your statement. A roof repair at UGX 600,000 plus our UGX 60,000 is
UGX 660,000, so we ask you first and you reply YES.

**Why** Mwavuli arranges the work as the landlord's agent and the contractor does it. Workmanship, trade licensing
and building liability stay with the contractor, and Mwavuli earns from a fee the landlord can see.

**Counsel note**

- Agent, not builder: confirm Mwavuli is not liable for workmanship beyond choosing and supervising the
  contractor, and that trade licensing (electrical, plumbing, building) sits with the contractors.
- The coordination fee is a benefit to the agent. Disclose it and get the landlord's agreement in writing so it
  is not a secret profit.
- Utilities and garbage at cost: confirm what the electricity regulator and the water utility allow when a
  landlord re-bills tenants, and how a share is set for a unit with no meter (a fair share or a fixed amount).
- Damage a tenant causes: the tenant pays once the repairs are done, with the deposit as the fallback (BM6).
  Confirm what the Landlord and Tenant Act 2022 allows, who pays the contractor first, and whether the
  coordination fee is charged to the tenant. Open.
- Construction scope is not settled: new buildings and extensions may need a separate works agreement. Open.

### BM5 · When a tenant has not paid

*Source: Owner. The covered-month claim and the oldest-month-first rule were proposed and accepted. That the
chasing starts on the missed due date was decided on 2026-11-13.*

**Plain statement** If a tenant misses the due date, we do the chasing for you from that day. We remind, call and
visit, then send a written notice. Should the matter need to go to court, the case is brought in your name, and
only with your YES. We never lock a tenant out. Our time costs you nothing extra. A lawyer, the court and a bailiff
are paid at cost: from the tenant's deposit first, then by you. The month we covered is ours to collect. When a
tenant pays what is owed, the oldest month is paid first, so the month we covered is repaid before later months
reach you.

**Example** A room rents for UGX 400,000, due on the 5th. On 1st March we pay you UGX 340,000 for it. The tenant
has not paid by 5th March, so we start chasing that day. On 1st April it is still unpaid: we send nothing for that
room. On 20th April the tenant pays UGX 400,000. It repays March, so we keep it (UGX 340,000 we advanced and
UGX 60,000 fee). April is still owed, so we still send nothing on 1st May. When April is paid, we send you
UGX 340,000 of it.

**Why** The landlord gets real help when a tenant stops paying. Chasing from the day a due date is missed gives
the tenant the earliest chance to pay, and Mwavuli the most time to collect before the cover stops on the next
1st. Mwavuli's own time is part of the 15%, and its loss on one room stops at one month's net rent. The landlord
carries the outside legal costs because the claim is the landlord's and the landlord decides whether to go to
court.

**Counsel note**

- Mwavuli acts as the landlord's agent. Legal steps are taken in the landlord's name and need the landlord's
  YES, recorded in writing or by message.
- The chasing starts on the missed due date and runs alongside the cover. The pause on the next 1st (BM1) is a
  separate test.
- When the written notice goes out is open: with the pause on the next 1st, or at the end of the visits. Confirm
  how soon after a missed due date the Landlord and Tenant Act 2022 lets a landlord or agent send a written notice,
  and what it must say. Open.
- The covered month passes to Mwavuli as a claim. Draft the assignment (or subrogation) and the
  oldest-month-first rule so a court reads them the same way.
- Who brings a case for the covered month is open: the landlord, in its name and with its YES (above), or Mwavuli, in
  its own right as the claim passes to it (BM1). Open.
- Mwavuli never locks a tenant out or removes belongings. Confirm the eviction steps and who may carry them out
  under the Landlord and Tenant Act 2022 and related rules.
- Legal costs come from the deposit where Mwavuli holds one (BM6). On an existing tenant's unit the landlord
  pays them directly.
- How a landlord says YES and how it is recorded are in P6.

## Money

### BM6 · Deposits

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** A new tenant's deposit is paid to us and kept in a separate account. We use it only for what
the tenancy allows (unpaid rent, damage, legal costs), and the rest goes back to the tenant. For tenants
already in the building, you keep the deposit you hold. We write the amount down when you sign up, and you
stay responsible for giving it back.

**Example** A new tenant pays a UGX 400,000 deposit to us and we keep it apart. At move-out UGX 50,000 is kept
for a broken door and UGX 350,000 goes back to the tenant. For a tenant already in the building, you hold
UGX 400,000. We note it on the file and it stays yours to return.

**Why** Mwavuli pays the landlord first, so a deposit is its cushion for the covered month and the first source
for legal costs. Keeping new deposits apart protects the tenant's money and the landlord's trust. Existing
deposits stay where they are so a landlord can join without handing over money.

**Counsel note**

- Mwavuli holds new deposits for the tenant. Confirm the Landlord and Tenant Act 2022 rules on the amount, how a
  deposit is held and when it is returned.
- Keep a register of each tenant's deposit and who holds it. A deposit a landlord holds cannot be used against
  a month Mwavuli has covered: proposed as a landlord duty (see Open).
- Units with existing tenants have no deposit cushion, so their legal costs fall on the landlord.

### BM17 · The payout account

*Source: Owner. The accept test was proposed and approved.*

**Plain statement** We pay your rent from an account we keep for rent payouts and use for nothing else. We take on
a building only when that account can cover its payouts, so the promise to pay you on the 1st can be kept.

**Example** A building of 10 rooms at UGX 400,000 has a net payout of UGX 3,400,000 a month. Before we accept it,
the account must hold two months of that, UGX 6,800,000, on top of what is already set aside for the buildings we
manage: one month for the payout and one for a covered month that is still unrecovered.

**Why** The promise to pay on the 1st rests on the money being there. A separate account makes the claim true and
checkable, and the accept test stops Mwavuli signing more than it can pay.

**Rule** Mwavuli accepts a building only if the payout account, after what is committed to buildings already
accepted, holds two months of the building's net rent. The float and the launch size are tracked outside the repo.

**Counsel note**

- Decide the account structure with the accountant: a collection account, the payout account, and the client
  money account for deposits and prepaid rent (BM6, P13). Confirm that client money is kept apart from Mwavuli's
  own funds.
- P9 lets Mwavuli decline a building it cannot fund. The agreement says so before the landlord signs.

## Taking on a building

### BM7 · Proof of ownership

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** Show us your title, or the papers on our list. We check them before we start. A certificate
of title in your name is enough. If you have no title, bring a sale agreement or letters of administration (or
probate), a letter from your local council (LC1), and your signed statement that you own the building and may
let it. We always need your national ID. Where there is a title, we search it. We do not take a building that
is in dispute, or that has co-owners who have not all agreed in writing.

**Example** A family building with no title: the landlord brings the sale agreement, an LC1 letter, a national ID
and a signed statement. Mwavuli keeps copies on file and starts once all four are checked.

**Why** Mwavuli pays the landlord and collects rent in the landlord's name, so it must know the person signing
can let the building. A tiered list keeps the market wide while the check stays real.

**Counsel note**

- Confirm what counts as enough proof for each kind of land the landlords hold (freehold, leasehold, mailo,
  customary) and how to run a title search.
- Check whether a mortgage or other charge on the building limits letting, and whether the lender must consent.
- Landlords acting for family owners or an estate need written authority from every owner, or letters of
  administration.

## Leaving

### BM8 · Ending the agreement

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** You can leave any time with 30 days' written notice, and so can we. If we have not paid you by
the 3rd, you can leave straight away, with no fee. We can end the agreement straight away if you collect rent from
tenants yourself, interfere with tenants, or refuse a repair that is urgent for safety.

**Example** The rent was due to you on 1st May and we have not paid it. On 4th May you tell us in writing that you
are leaving. The agreement ends that day and you owe no fee.

**Why** A landlord who has never met Mwavuli will fear being stuck with it. No lock-in is the strongest trust
signal, and the right to leave at once makes missing the 1st costly for Mwavuli.

**Counsel note**

- There is no minimum term. Notice is 30 days either way. Confirm how notice is given (in writing, by message) and
  from when it counts.
- Late payment: define "not paid" as the money not having reached the landlord's account or mobile money number by
  the end of the 3rd.
- Define each reason Mwavuli may leave at once (taking rent directly, interfering with tenants, refusing an urgent
  safety repair) so a landlord can recognize it.
- Exit mechanics (the final statement, tenant files, deposits and the covered-month claim) are in P8.

## Who Mwavuli serves

### BM9 · Homes and business premises, in one area

*Source: Owner. Kampala and its surroundings, confirmed by the owner.*

**Plain statement** We manage homes and business premises in Kampala and its surroundings: rooms, flats, small
blocks, shops and offices. There is no minimum number of units.

**Example** A landlord with six rooms and one shop gives us all seven units. All seven get the same terms.

**Why** The brand speaks to people and businesses, and the landlords expected to sign own both. One area keeps
staff visits, trusted contractors, lawyers and garbage collection close. There is no minimum size, because P9 lets
Mwavuli decline a building it cannot fund.

**Counsel note**

- Commercial tenancies run longer and carry different terms. Prepare a commercial tenancy form to sit under the
  same landlord agreement.
- VAT on commercial rent and withholding tax on rent: confirm who files, and that the 15% is worked out on rent
  excluding VAT.
- Rent currency: confirm whether rent may be quoted in US dollars and how the guarantee and the payout work if so.

### BM10 · One set of terms for every unit

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** Every unit we take on gets the same terms, whatever its rent, shops and offices included: we
take 15% of its rent, we cover its first missed month, and we pay you on the 1st.

**Example** A shop rents for UGX 3,000,000 and the tenant does not pay in March. On 1st March you receive
UGX 2,550,000 (the rent minus our 15%), and we collect March from the tenant.

**Why** One set of terms is the simplest pitch and one agreement to explain.

**Counsel note**

- One large unit that does not pay costs Mwavuli its whole net rent for the covered month: UGX 2,550,000 for a
  shop that rents at UGX 3,000,000. That is as much as the fees from about 40 rooms at UGX 400,000.
- The payout account has to cover the largest units (BM17). P9 lets Mwavuli decline a unit it cannot fund.

## Signing

### BM11 · Languages and signing

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** The agreement comes in English and Luganda. You choose one, and you and we sign that version.
One of our officers sits down with you, explains the agreement in your language and answers your questions. You
sign, or give your thumbprint, in front of a witness you choose. You keep a copy.

**Example** A landlord reads Luganda but not English. Our officer sits down with the landlord at the building and
explains the Luganda version. The landlord signs that version with a sister as witness and
leaves with a copy.

**Why** If a landlord later says "I did not understand", Mwavuli can show that the terms were explained in the
landlord's own language, with a witness the landlord chose.

**Counsel note**

- The Luganda text is a complete translation, reviewed by a qualified person, and reviewed again whenever the
  terms change (brand F8).
- Only one version is signed. Decide how the agreement says that the signed version governs and what the other
  version is for. A landlord who signed after an explanation in Luganda should not lose on an English
  technicality.
- The Illiterates Protection Act may require whoever writes a document for a landlord who cannot read it to put
  their full name and address on it, as a statement that it was read over and explained. The set has no
  explanation note: confirm whether the agreement needs that statement and what it must say.
- Decide who may witness and what Mwavuli records about the witness.

## Defaults

### BM12 · The mechanics

*Source: Owner. Proposed in the interview and approved by the owner. P14 and P15 were decided on
2026-11-13, except two proposals in P15: the channel for a tenant's notice, and counting the 30 days from the day we
learn the tenant has gone (see Open).*

- **P1 Paying and statements.** Tenants pay only Mwavuli, by mobile money or bank, never cash to staff. The
  landlord gets one statement a month by SMS or WhatsApp in the chosen language, with paper on request.
- **P2 "Occupied".** A tenant living in the unit under our tenancy form, with the rent and deposit written
  down. The first-month cover starts on the first 1st after that.
- **P3 Existing tenants.** Each moves onto our form after a visit. Rent owed before sign-up is not covered; we
  collect it for the same 15%.
- **P4 Inspection.** We walk the building with the landlord first and take photos. Unsafe items must be fixed
  within 30 days, before we start. We may decline a building that stays unsafe.
- **P5 Landlord duties.** Tell tenants to pay only us, and pass on any rent paid to you the same day. No side
  deals on rent or tenants. No lock-outs, and no cutting water or power. Let us in with notice. Your taxes and
  rates stay yours. Tell us the truth about ownership, debts and disputes.
- **P6 Saying YES.** A reply by SMS or WhatsApp, or a recorded call confirmed by text. Urgent safety repairs up
  to UGX 500,000 go ahead first, and we tell you the same day.
- **P7 Fees.** Only the 15% on rent and the coordination fee on repair quotes. No sign-up, letting or yearly
  fee, and none charged to tenants. Late fees written in the tenancy form go to Mwavuli.
- **P8 On exit.** A final statement and payment within 14 days of the end date. It pays the rent we collected
  and haven't paid out, plus prepaid rent for rent months that start after the end date, in full with no 15%, except
  rent that goes back to a tenant whose tenancy has ended (P15), less costs you approved and any covered month a
  tenant still owes us. Tenant files go to the landlord. A deposit we hold goes to the landlord after any rent the
  tenant still owes is taken from it, and tenants are told.
- **P9 Right to decline.** We may decline a building or unit we cannot fund, or that fails the ownership
  check or the inspection. Once we accept it, the promise applies.
- **P10 VAT on commercial rent.** VAT a tenant pays on rent is not part of the rent for the 15%. We pass it
  to the landlord in the same payment and show it on the statement. Filing it is the landlord's job.
- **P11 Privacy.** Landlord and tenant details are used only to manage the building and are not sold or
  shared otherwise.
- **P12 Disagreements.** We talk first, then a mediator we both accept, and only then the courts of Uganda.
- **P13 Prepaid rent.** Held in a separate account, like deposits, and paid out month by month on the 1st, minus
  15%. What has not been paid out stays there. If a tenant leaves, the rent still held for rent months after the
  tenancy ends goes back to the tenant as P15 says, with no fee on them. At exit, each tenant gets a note, "rent
  paid up to [date]", and the landlord honors it.
- **P14 Part months.** A rent month runs from one due date of a tenancy to the next. For part months, every month
  counts as 30 days, so each day is one-thirtieth of the monthly rent. A new tenant pays for the days from move-in to
  the first due date when moving in, and we pay that rent to the landlord, minus our 15%, on the first payout after
  move-in. A tenant whose due date moves pays for the days counted forward from the old due date to the new one. In
  each case the amount for the days is rounded to the nearest UGX 1,000, a half rounding up. A rent month that has
  started is paid in full: a tenant who leaves part-way through it gets none of it back.
- **P15 A tenant leaving.** A tenant on our tenancy form gives us 30 days' notice, by phone, SMS or WhatsApp, and
  we confirm it by message. The tenancy ends on the day before a due date, at least 30 days after we get the
  notice, so the last rent month is always whole. A tenant who leaves without the notice owes the rent until the
  tenancy would have ended, counted from the day we learn the tenant has gone, and we take it from the deposit
  first, if we hold it. We visit the unit when the tenancy ends, or when we learn the tenant has gone. Within 14 days
  of that visit, the rent still held for rent months that start after the tenancy ends goes back to the tenant, once
  we have taken what the tenant still owes from the deposit first, if we hold it, and then from this rent.
