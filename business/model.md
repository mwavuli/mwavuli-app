# Mwavuli business model

Status: version 0.1, draft for owner review. Ticket SNT-25. Interview started on 2026-11-10.
Interview transcript: [grill-me session](../.scratch/grill-me/1794346863-mwavuli-business-model.md).

What Mwavuli Property Management offers a landlord, what it charges and what each side does. It is the single
source for the landlord agreements and for the pitch to prospective landlords. It is not legal advice: counsel
reviews every term before an agreement is used.

## How to read

Each term has an ID (BM1, BM2), a source, a **Plain statement**, an **Example**, a **Why** and a **Counsel note**.

- The **Plain statement** is the wording a landlord hears and reads. The agreements and the pitch take it as
  written.
- The **Counsel note** is for the lawyer and the accountant. It is not landlord-facing text.

| Source   | Meaning                                                  |
|----------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Owner    | Decided by the owner during the SNT-25 interview         |
| Proposed | Added to complete the model; open until the owner agrees |

Examples use round, illustrative figures in UGX. They show how a rule works; they are not quotes.

## Audience

### BM3 · Who the offer is written for

*Source: Owner*

The main customer is a landlord in Uganda with little formal education, little knowledge of the law and little
patience for legal language.

**Rules**

- Every plain statement passes the read-aloud test: a staff member can say it to a landlord in one breath and the
  landlord can say it back.
- Every plain statement carries an example in UGX with round figures.
- The offer carries few numbers and they are round, so a landlord can remember all of them.
- Landlord documents are short. The full legal terms sit in a schedule behind them, and the plain statements say
  what they mean.
- Counsel notes stay out of landlord-facing text.

**Why** A landlord who cannot follow a term cannot give informed consent to it, and an agreement the landlord
did not understand is the first one to be disputed. Plain terms are also what make the pitch believable.

**Counsel note**

- The Illiterates Protection Act may require a signed explanation when a document is given to someone who cannot
  read the language it is written in. Confirm what it requires and how to meet it at signing.
- Ambiguous wording is usually read against the party that drafted it, which is Mwavuli. Plain drafting protects
  Mwavuli too.

## The offer

### BM1 · Rent on the 1st

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** For every unit with a tenant living in it, you get the rent on the 1st of the month. If the
tenant has not paid, we still pay you for that month. If the tenant has still not paid by the next 1st, we stop
paying for that unit until the tenant has paid up or a new tenant moves in.

**Example** A room rents for UGX 400,000 and the tenant does not pay in March. On 1 Mar you receive UGX 340,000 for
it (the rent minus our 15%). On 1 Apr the rent is still unpaid, so we send nothing for that room until it is
settled.

**Why** A cap turns an open-ended liability into one month per unit that Mwavuli can fund and a lawyer can write,
and the promise stays true for every month inside it.

**Pitch wording** "Rent on the 1st for every occupied unit. If a tenant misses, we cover the first month." Never
"always paid".

**Counsel note**

- The cap is per unit and resets when the arrears are cleared.
- When Mwavuli pays a month the tenant has not paid, the claim for that rent passes to Mwavuli. Draft it so
  Mwavuli can collect that month in its own right.
- A paid guarantee of rent may count as insurance or suretyship. Confirm whether it needs a license or an
  approval and how to word it.
- "Occupied" is not yet defined. Open (onboarding).

### BM4 · What we charge and how you are paid

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** We take 15% of the rent. No rent, no fee. On the 1st we send you the rent of every unit with a
tenant, minus our 15%, in one payment with one statement. Costs you have approved are taken off the same payment
and listed on the statement. A job never takes more than one payout: for a bigger job, you pay the balance before
work starts. The 15% is charged on rent only: not on deposits, not on utility or garbage money and not on repair
costs.

**Example** A room rents for UGX 400,000. Our 15% is UGX 60,000. You receive UGX 340,000 on the 1st.

**Why** One payment and one statement mean a landlord never has a bill to pay and can check the month at a
glance.

**Counsel note**

- Fee base: rent only, excluding deposits, pass-through costs and the works coordination fee.
- In a covered month (BM1) Mwavuli pays out 85% of the rent and earns its 15% only if that rent is collected
  later.
- For the accountant: VAT on the fee, withholding tax on the fee, and whether the pitch can call 15% the final
  price.
- A job never takes more than one payout, so the 1st payout stays whole and Mwavuli does not lend. Confirm how the
  landlord pays the balance (to the contractor or to Mwavuli) and how it is recorded.

### BM2 · Repairs and building work

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** When something needs fixing or building, we find a trusted contractor and bring you the price.
You pay the contractor's price plus our coordination fee of 10%, shown on the quote. Above UGX 200,000 on the quote
we wait for your YES before work starts. Electricity, water and garbage bills are paid at cost, with no markup.

**Example** A plumber quotes UGX 100,000 to fix a tap. Our 10% is UGX 10,000, so the quote is UGX 110,000. That is
below UGX 200,000, so we fix it and list it on your statement. A roof repair at UGX 600,000 plus our UGX 60,000 is
UGX 660,000, so we ask you first and you reply YES.

**Why** Mwavuli arranges the work as the landlord's agent and the contractor does it. Workmanship, trade licensing
and building liability stay with the contractor, and Mwavuli earns from a fee the landlord can see.

**Counsel note**

- Agent, not builder: confirm Mwavuli is not liable for workmanship beyond choosing and supervising the
  contractor, and that trade licensing (electrical, plumbing, building) sits with the contractors.
- The coordination fee is a benefit to the agent. Disclose it and get the landlord's agreement in writing so it
  is not a secret profit.
- Utilities and garbage at cost: confirm what the electricity regulator and the water utility allow when a
  landlord re-bills tenants.
- Construction scope is not settled: new buildings and extensions may need a separate works agreement. Open.

### BM5 · When a tenant still has not paid

*Source: Owner. The covered-month claim and the oldest-month-first rule were proposed and accepted.*

**Plain statement** If a tenant still has not paid after the month we covered, we do the chasing for you. We
remind, call and visit, then send a written notice. If it must go to court, we ask you first and you say YES.
We never lock a tenant out. Our time costs you nothing extra. A lawyer, the court and a bailiff are paid at
cost: from the tenant's deposit first, then by you. The month we covered is ours to collect. When a tenant
pays what is owed, the oldest month is paid first, so the month we covered is repaid before later months
reach you.

**Example** A room rents for UGX 400,000. The tenant does not pay in March, so we pay you UGX 340,000 on 1 Mar.
On 1 Apr it is still unpaid: we send nothing for that room and give the tenant a written notice. On 20 Apr the
tenant pays UGX 400,000. It repays March, so we keep it (UGX 340,000 we advanced and UGX 60,000 fee). April is
still owed, so we still send nothing on 1 May. When April is paid, we send you UGX 340,000 of it.

**Why** The landlord gets real help when a tenant stops paying. Mwavuli's own time is part of the 15%, and its
loss on one room stops at one month's net rent. The landlord carries the outside legal costs because the claim
is the landlord's and the landlord decides whether to go to court.

**Counsel note**

- Mwavuli acts as the landlord's agent. Legal steps are taken in the landlord's name and need the landlord's
  YES, recorded in writing or by message.
- The covered month passes to Mwavuli as a claim. Draft the assignment (or subrogation) and the
  oldest-month-first rule so a court reads them the same way.
- Mwavuli never locks a tenant out or removes belongings. Confirm the eviction steps and who may carry them out
  under the Landlord and Tenant Act 2022 and related rules.
- Legal costs come from the deposit where Mwavuli holds one (BM6). On an existing tenant's unit the landlord
  pays them directly.
- How a landlord says YES and how it is recorded: open (duties).

## Money

### BM6 · Deposits

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** A new tenant's deposit is paid to us and kept in a separate account. We use it only for what
the tenancy allows (unpaid rent, damage, legal costs), and the rest goes back to the tenant. For tenants
already in the building, you keep the deposit you hold. We write the amount down when you sign up, and you
stay responsible for giving it back.

**Example** A new tenant pays a UGX 400,000 deposit to us and we keep it apart. At move-out UGX 50,000 is kept
for a broken door and UGX 350,000 goes back to the tenant. For a tenant already in the building, you hold
UGX 400,000. We note it on the file and it stays yours to return.

**Why** Mwavuli pays the landlord first, so a deposit is its cushion for the covered month and the first source
for legal costs. Keeping new deposits apart protects the tenant's money and the landlord's trust. Existing
deposits stay where they are so a landlord can join without handing over money.

**Counsel note**

- Mwavuli holds new deposits for the tenant. Confirm the Landlord and Tenant Act 2022 rules on the amount, how a
  deposit is held and when it is returned.
- Keep a register of each tenant's deposit and who holds it. A deposit a landlord holds cannot be used against
  a month Mwavuli has covered: write this into the landlord's duties (open).
- Units with existing tenants have no deposit cushion, so their legal costs fall on the landlord.

## Taking on a building

### BM7 · Proof of ownership

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** Show us your title, or the papers on our list. We check them before we start. A certificate
of title in your name is enough. If you have no title, bring a sale agreement or letters of administration (or
probate), a letter from your local council (LC1), and your signed statement that you own the building and may
let it. We always need your national ID. Where there is a title, we search it. We do not take a building that
is in dispute, or that has co-owners who have not all agreed in writing.

**Example** A family building with no title: the landlord brings the sale agreement, an LC1 letter, a national ID
and a signed statement. Mwavuli keeps copies on file and starts once all four are checked.

**Why** Mwavuli pays the landlord and collects rent in the landlord's name, so it must know the person signing
can let the building. A tiered list keeps the market wide while the check stays real.

**Counsel note**

- Confirm what counts as enough proof for each kind of land the landlords hold (freehold, leasehold, mailo,
  customary) and how to run a title search.
- Check whether a mortgage or other charge on the building limits letting, and whether the lender must consent.
- Landlords acting for family owners or an estate need written authority from every owner, or letters of
  administration.
- Open: what "occupied" means, existing tenants and the building inspection (onboarding).

## Leaving

### BM8 · Ending the agreement

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** You can leave any time with 30 days' written notice, and so can we. If we have not paid you by
the 3rd, you can leave straight away, with no fee. We can end the agreement straight away if you collect rent from
tenants yourself, interfere with tenants, or refuse a repair that is urgent for safety.

**Example** The rent was due to you on 1 May and we have not paid it. On 4 May you tell us in writing that you are
leaving. The agreement ends that day and you owe no fee.

**Why** A landlord who has never met Mwavuli will fear being stuck with it. No lock-in is the strongest trust
signal, and the right to leave at once makes missing the 1st costly for Mwavuli.

**Counsel note**

- There is no minimum term. Notice is 30 days either way. Confirm how notice is given (in writing, by message) and
  from when it counts.
- Late payment: define "not paid" as the money not having reached the landlord's account or mobile money number by
  the end of the 3rd. The day is a number for the numbers round.
- Define each reason Mwavuli may leave at once (taking rent directly, interfering with tenants, refusing an urgent
  safety repair) so a landlord can recognize it.
- Exit mechanics (the final statement, tenant files, deposits and the covered-month claim) are in P8.

## Who Mwavuli serves

### BM9 · Homes and business premises, in one area

*Source: Owner. The area was not named; Kampala and its surroundings are assumed.*

**Plain statement** We manage homes and business premises in Kampala and its surroundings: rooms, flats, small
blocks, shops and offices. There is no minimum number of units.

**Example** A landlord with six rooms and one shop gives us all seven units. All seven get the same terms.

**Why** The brand speaks to people and businesses, and the landlords expected to sign own both. One area keeps
staff visits, trusted contractors, lawyers and garbage collection close. There is no minimum size, because P9 lets
Mwavuli decline a building it cannot fund.

**Counsel note**

- Commercial tenancies run longer and carry different terms. Prepare a commercial tenancy form to sit under the
  same landlord agreement.
- VAT on commercial rent and withholding tax on rent: confirm who files, and that the 15% is worked out on rent
  excluding VAT.
- Rent currency: confirm whether rent may be quoted in US dollars and how the guarantee and the payout work if so.

### BM10 · One set of terms for every unit

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** A shop or an office gets the same terms as a room: our 15%, the first month covered, and your
rent on the 1st. No unit is left out because its rent is large.

**Example** A shop rents for UGX 3,000,000 and the tenant does not pay in March. On 1 Mar you receive UGX 2,550,000
(the rent minus our 15%), and we collect March from the tenant.

**Why** One set of terms is the simplest pitch and one agreement to explain.

**Counsel note**

- One large unit that does not pay costs Mwavuli its whole net rent for the covered month: UGX 2,550,000 for a
  shop that rents at UGX 3,000,000. That is as much as the fees from about 40 rooms at UGX 400,000.
- Mwavuli should be able to decline a building or unit it cannot fund when it signs up. Once accepted, the promise
  applies. Proposed default.
- The float is sized for the largest units: numbers round.

## Signing

### BM11 · Languages and signing

*Source: Owner*

**Plain statement** The agreement comes in English and Luganda, and you sign both. One of our officers reads
the main points to you out loud, in your language, and answers your questions. You sign, or give your
thumbprint, in front of a witness you choose. A short signed note says who explained it to you and that you
understood. You keep a copy.

**Example** A landlord reads Luganda but not English. Our officer reads the Luganda version aloud at the
building. The landlord signs both versions with a sister as witness, and the officer signs the note. The
landlord leaves with both copies.

**Why** If a landlord later says "I did not understand", Mwavuli can show that the terms were explained in the
landlord's own language, with a witness the landlord chose.

**Counsel note**

- The Luganda text is a complete translation, reviewed by a qualified person, and reviewed again whenever the
  terms change (brand F8).
- Decide which version governs if the two differ, and how the agreement says so. A landlord who signed after an
  explanation in Luganda should not lose on an English technicality.
- The Illiterates Protection Act may require the signed explanation note. Confirm what it requires and what the
  note must say.
- Decide who may witness and what Mwavuli records about the witness.

## Defaults

### BM12 · The mechanics

*Source: Owner. Proposed in the interview and approved by the owner.*

- **P1 Paying and statements.** Tenants pay only Mwavuli, by mobile money or bank, never cash to staff. The
  landlord gets one statement a month by SMS or WhatsApp in the chosen language, with paper on request.
- **P2 "Occupied".** A tenant living in the unit under our tenancy form, with the rent and deposit written
  down. The first-month cover starts on the first 1st after that.
- **P3 Existing tenants.** Each moves onto our form after a visit. Rent owed before sign-up is not covered; we
  collect it for the same 15%.
- **P4 Inspection.** We walk the building with the landlord first and take photos. Unsafe items must be fixed
  within 30 days, before we start. We may decline a building that stays unsafe.
- **P5 Landlord duties.** Tell tenants to pay only us, and pass on any rent paid to you the same day. No side
  deals on rent or tenants. No lock-outs, and no cutting water or power. Let us in with notice. Your taxes and
  rates stay yours. Tell us the truth about ownership, debts and disputes.
- **P6 Saying YES.** A reply by SMS or WhatsApp, or a recorded call confirmed by text. Urgent safety repairs up
  to UGX 500,000 go ahead first, and we tell you the same day.
- **P7 Fees.** Only the 15% on rent and the coordination fee on repair quotes. No sign-up, letting or yearly
  fee, and none charged to tenants. Late fees written in the tenancy form go to Mwavuli.
- **P8 On exit.** A final statement and payment within 14 days of the end date. It pays the rent we collected
  and haven't paid out, plus prepaid rent for months after the end date in full with no 15%, less costs you
  approved and any covered month a tenant still owes us. Tenant files go to the landlord. A deposit we hold
  goes to the landlord after any rent the tenant still owes is taken from it, and tenants are told.
- **P9 Right to decline.** We may decline a building or unit we cannot fund, or that fails the ownership
  check or the inspection. Once we accept it, the promise applies.
- **P10 VAT on commercial rent.** VAT a tenant pays on rent is not part of the rent for the 15%. We pass it
  to the landlord in the same payment and show it on the statement. Filing it is the landlord's job.
- **P11 Privacy.** Landlord and tenant details are used only to manage the building and are not sold or
  shared otherwise.
- **P12 Disagreements.** We talk first, then a mediator we both accept, and only then the courts of Uganda.
- **P13 Prepaid rent.** Held in a separate account, like deposits, and paid out month by month on the 1st, minus
  15%. Months that haven't started stay there. If a tenant leaves early, unused months go back to the tenant under
  the tenancy form, with no fee on them. At exit, each tenant gets a note, "rent paid up to [month]", and the
  landlord honors it.

**Counsel note**

- P1: confirm whether collecting and holding rent for landlords needs a payment-service approval, and whether
  Mwavuli is an accountable person under the Anti-Money Laundering Act, with customer checks and records to
  match.
- P2, P3: write "occupied" in the tenancy form and the agreement in the same words. Old arrears Mwavuli collects
  carry the same 15%; confirm the wording.
- P4: define "unsafe" as a short list (roof, wiring, water, stairs) and keep the signed inspection report.
- P5: breach of a landlord duty lets Mwavuli end the agreement at once (BM8). Confirm the landlord's tax duties
  with the accountant, and the rules on cutting services and lock-outs.
- P6: confirm how SMS and WhatsApp replies and recorded calls are kept and relied on as consent, including
  consent to the recording.
- P7: confirm what late fees the Landlord and Tenant Act 2022 lets a landlord or agent charge a tenant.
- P8: confirm the rules on handing over deposits and the tenant's rights.
- P9: record the reason each time a building or unit is declined.
- P10: the accountant confirms the VAT treatment, the landlord's VAT status and who issues tax invoices.
- P11: confirm duties under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 (registration, consent notices, retention).
- P12: confirm governing law, the mediation step and the courts with jurisdiction.
- P13: confirm that prepaid rent held for landlords and tenants is held in trust, kept apart from Mwavuli's own
  funds and safe from its creditors, and that the tenancy form says whether unused prepaid months are refundable
  when a tenant leaves early.
