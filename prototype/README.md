# QUICK LINKS (SNT-38, prototype 13)

The page starts with what each visitor came to do: four large tiles on a Green field, each jumping to one short answer. No portal, no login, no JavaScript.

## References and the moves borrowed
- Invitation Homes: quick actions laid over the hero (icon over label, loud); a slim strip above a clean header; the hero is its own field.
- Grace Property Management: role routes are the navigation (owners and investors, residents); hairline rhythm of short answers.
- hello Management: loud header buttons (outline plus filled); a row of big role tiles straight after the headline.

## Distinctive
A three-line giant headline over layered flat roof bands at 43 degrees; the photo slot sits on a roof whose apex is always hidden behind it;
144 px numerals (1st, 15%, 30 days); cards overlapping a sage band; a gold rule that draws under a heading only when its answer is reached.
Five sections and a footer on Green, white, sage, deep Green and cream, none built alike. About five desktop screens.

## Brand rules that shaped it
L1, L4, L5 (195 px lockup, 24 px clear); C2 to C4 (gold only on Green for large text, marks and rules); T1, T2, T5, T9; S3, S4, S5, S8, S9;
I1, I5, I7, I8, I10; M1 to M4, M6; F1, F5, F6; A5, A6. Skeleton defects fixed: lockups h-12, buttons rounded-md and 48 px.

## Imagery strategy
Pictograms on Green badges, big numerals, flat roof bands, and one photo slot (D14): 4:3 on tablets and desktops, a wide crop on phones.
Its planes (cream sky with the M2 pattern, sage roofs, the building with the mark's four-square window per L2) are flat tones; the caption sits on a solid chip.

## Deliberate bends
- S2 (Default): page margin is 24 px on phones, not 16, so the 195 px lockup keeps its 24 px L4 clear space.
- T3 (Avoid): the owners view uses more than four sizes (numerals 144, title 56, 32, 18, 14, 12) because the numerals are the answer. The first view keeps to four (88, 20, 16, 14).
- Reading to confirm, not a bend: I7 bars pictograms for interface actions. Here a pictogram names the topic of a tile or answer; the action cue is the line `arrow-right`.

## Placeholders
Photograph (D14); "Mobile money number to be added"; "Bank account to be added"; "Phone or WhatsApp number to be added"; "Email address to be added". No `tel:` or `mailto:` links. Forms: none.
