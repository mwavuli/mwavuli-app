# Calm authority (SNT-38, prototype 01)
An institutional property manager that feels unhurried and sure of itself: big plain type, flat Green fields, a lot of air.
## References and moves borrowed
- FirstService Residential: a centred statement over a full-bleed field with one outlined button; three audience cards overlap the hero's lower edge.
- FirstService Residential: three tall image cards straddle a colour split, the middle one lower, with short captions; a multi-column footer.
- Savills: a large plain headline with air and one prompt line; a band of oversize figures, here the three terms (1st, 15%, 30 days), not statistics.
- Both: quiet navigation, and no two neighbouring sections built alike.
## Distinctive
Layered flat roof bands at 43 degrees bleed off both sides of the hero. The contact panel overlaps the footer as the cards overlap the hero. Every photo slot is art-directed from the roof form and the mark's window grid.
## Brand rules that shaped it
L4 and L5 (lockup 195 px, 24 px clear), F1, F6 and A1 (tagline; the footer's top row is the card back's), M1 to M4 (roof bands, pattern, one gold rule), C2 to C4, T1 to T3 (display scaled to 80 px for the hero), T5, T9, S3, S4, S8, S9, I5, I7, I8, A5, A6.
## Imagery strategy
No photograph exists (D14). Five photo slots: the wide hero (flat Green, roof bands, no pattern, per M2) and four light slots (the statement band and three 3:4 service cards). Each has its own flat planes, the M2 pattern on its light ground and the caption "Photograph to come (D14)".
## Deliberate bends
- S2: page margin is 24 px on phones (S2 says 16) so the 195 px lockup keeps its L4 clear space and every block shares its left edge.
- A5: the outline button is white on the Green hero; a Green outline cannot show on Green.
- M4: the hero's gold rule is centred under the centred headline, not left-aligned.
- T3: six type sizes on the first screen (hero, h3, body-lg, body, label, caption).
- C2: four gold elements in the hero (two 8 px band stripes, the second headline beat, the rule); the stripes are about a thirtieth of each motif (M3 allows a fifth).
- M2: roofs on the green-100 and sage-100 slots are 8 and 7 percent Green mixed into the ground (about 1.1 to 1), not the cream value.
- I8: contact badges are Green squares at 20 percent radius with line-family glyphs for all three rows, one weight; BG1 and BG2 are not used.
